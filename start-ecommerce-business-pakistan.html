How to Start an E-Commerce Business in Pakistan: A Step-by-Step Guide (2026)

By Closed 360 · Updated 11 October 2026 · 9 min read

Starting an online business in Pakistan comes down to five things: a product people want, a supplier you can trust, a way to take orders, a way to deliver them, and a way to find customers. This guide walks through each one in order, with the numbers you should check before you spend money.

1. How online selling works in Pakistan today

Most small online sellers in Pakistan reach buyers through social media (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), close the order on WhatsApp or a simple store, and deliver by courier with cash on delivery. Marketplaces such as Daraz are another route, with their own rules, fees and seller requirements.

About TikTok Shop: a Pakistani seller-guide published in August 2026 reported that in-app TikTok Shop checkout is not open to Pakistan-registered sellers. TikTok still works as a place to show products and reach buyers. Check TikTok's official Seller Center for the current status, and be careful with anyone who sells you a "TikTok Shop account" for a fee.

The practical lesson: choose the sales channel you can control, and treat social platforms as your shop window.

2. Choose a niche and your first product

You do not need a perfect product, you need one you can test cheaply. A good first product usually has these traits:

  • Easy to ship: small, light and not fragile.
  • Clear problem or benefit: you can explain it in a 15-second video.
  • Room in the price: after every cost, there is still profit (see step 4).
  • Easy to get again: more than one supplier can supply it.
  • Allowed: avoid medicines, counterfeit or fake-branded goods and anything restricted. Marketplaces and ad platforms can ban you for it.

Check demand before you buy: search the product on Instagram, Facebook and marketplaces, look at how many sellers have it and at what price, and read what buyers complain about. Complaints show you what to do better.

3. Find a supplier and order samples

In Karachi, many sellers start by sourcing from local wholesale markets because they can inspect goods, negotiate and restock quickly. Read our guide to sourcing from Karachi's wholesale markets for a visit checklist. Online wholesale platforms and direct manufacturers are other options.

  • Compare at least three suppliers on price, quality and minimum order.
  • Always order samples first, and test them yourself.
  • Ask what happens with defective items and whether you get a bill or receipt.

4. Work out your profit per order

Many new sellers price a product by looking only at the supplier rate. Instead, add up every cost for one order:

Profit per order = selling price − (product cost + packaging + delivery charge + advertising cost per order + allowance for returns and failed deliveries + payment or COD fees).

5. Set up your store

Start with the simplest setup that lets you take orders reliably:

  • Social page plus WhatsApp: fastest and cheapest to begin.
  • Your own online store (for example Shopify): more professional and easier to track orders.
  • Marketplace listing: access to existing shoppers, with platform rules and fees.

Whichever you choose, have clear photos, a plain price, delivery time, return policy and a visible WhatsApp number. Trust is the main thing buyers look for in a new shop.

6. Take orders and payments

Offer cash on delivery, and bank or mobile-wallet transfer such as JazzCash or Easypaisa if you are set up for them. Confirm every COD order by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Confirming the address, quantity and price removes many fake and mistaken orders. Keep a simple sheet of orders, payments and delivery status.

7. Delivery and returns

Compare courier services such as Trax, Leopards, Call Courier, PostEx or TCS on rates, pickup, COD payout time, tracking and how they handle returns. Test two couriers with a few parcels before committing. Pack so the product survives the trip, and photograph the parcel before dispatch in case of disputes. Track your return rate by courier and by city, so you can fix the weak spots.

8. Get your first customers

  • Short videos: show the product being used, not just displayed.
  • A small ad test: spend a small amount you can afford to lose, learn what works, then adjust. Do not scale before the numbers in step 4 work.
  • WhatsApp follow-up: thank buyers, ask for a review, and tell them about new items.
  • Reviews and photos from real buyers: ask for them and show them.

9. Registration and tax basics

What you need depends on how you operate, how much you sell and where you sell. Before you scale, check current requirements with FBR and SECP and talk to a qualified accountant. Keep records of purchases, sales and expenses from day one. This guide is not legal or tax advice.

10. Common mistakes to avoid

  • Copying a viral product without checking profit after ads, delivery and returns.
  • Ordering a large quantity before testing samples and demand.
  • Skipping order confirmation on COD orders.
  • Mixing personal and business money, so you never know your real profit.
  • Spending on ads before the product page and the offer are clear.

11. Your first 30 days

  1. Week 1: research the market and shortlist 3 products.
  2. Week 2: get samples, quotes and delivery rates, then do the profit sheet.
  3. Week 3: set up your store or page, shoot photos and videos, write your policies.
  4. Week 4: run a small test, confirm orders, ship, collect feedback, and decide whether to continue, change or stop.

12. Frequently asked questions

How much money do I need to start an e-commerce business in Pakistan?

There is no fixed amount. Start by testing one product with a budget you can afford to lose: a few sample units, packaging, and a small test of content or ads. Work out your cost per order first so you know how many orders you need before you break even.

Do I need a registered company to sell online in Pakistan?

Requirements depend on your business structure, your sales volume and the platform you use. Check current rules with FBR, SECP and a qualified accountant before you scale. This guide is not legal or tax advice.

Can I start an online store without buying stock?

Yes. Some sellers list a supplier's products and order only after a customer confirms, often called dropshipping. Margins can be thinner, delivery slower and quality harder to control, so test with your own samples first.

Is cash on delivery (COD) necessary in Pakistan?

COD is still the most common payment choice for many buyers, so most new stores offer it. Confirm every order by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch to reduce fake and impulse orders.

Is TikTok Shop available to sellers in Pakistan?

In August 2026, a Pakistani seller-guide reported that in-app TikTok Shop checkout was not open to Pakistan-registered sellers. TikTok can still be used to show products and reach buyers, with orders taken on WhatsApp or your own store. Check TikTok's official Seller Center for the current status before paying anyone for a 'TikTok Shop account'.
